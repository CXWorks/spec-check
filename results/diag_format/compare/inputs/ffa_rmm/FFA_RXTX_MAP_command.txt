13.6 FFA_RXTX_MAP command
               Maps the RX/TX buffer pair in the translation regime of the callee on behalf of an endpoint or Hypervisor.
               The buffer pair is described as follows:
                 • An SP describes to the SPM the VA or IPA contiguous pages allocated for each buffer in the pair.
                 • A VM describes to the Hypervisor the VA or IPA contiguous pages allocated for each buffer in the pair.
                 • A Hypervisor or OS Kernel describes to the SPM the physically contiguous pages allocated for each buffer in the pair.
                 • A Hypervisor forwards to the SPM the description of the pages that a VM allocated for each buffer in the pair.
