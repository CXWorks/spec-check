B4.5.41 RMI_REALM_DESTROY command
                 Destroys a Realm.
                 The RMI_REALM_DESTROY command may initiate a Stateful RMI Operation whose context is bound to the
                 object identified by rd.
                 The RMI_REALM_DESTROY command may initiate a memory-transferring RMI Operation.
