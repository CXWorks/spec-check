B4.5.57 RMI_RTT_AUX_PROT_UNMAP command
                  Remove mappings within a contiguous range of Protected IPA space from an auxiliary RTT tree.
                  The RMI_RTT_AUX_PROT_UNMAP command may initiate a Stateful RMI Operation whose context is bound to
                  the object identified by rd.
                  See also:
