B4.5.44 RMI_REC_DESTROY command
                 Destroys a REC.
                 The RMI_REC_DESTROY command may initiate a Stateful RMI Operation whose context is bound to the object
                 identified by rec_ptr.
                 The RMI_REC_DESTROY command may initiate a memory-transferring RMI Operation.
