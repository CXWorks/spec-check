B4.5.89 RMI_VSMMU_DESTROY command
                 Destroy a VSMMU.
                 The RMI_VSMMU_DESTROY command may initiate a Stateful RMI Operation whose context is bound to the
                 object identified by vsmmu_ptr.
                 The RMI_VSMMU_DESTROY command may initiate a memory-transferring RMI Operation.
                 See also:
