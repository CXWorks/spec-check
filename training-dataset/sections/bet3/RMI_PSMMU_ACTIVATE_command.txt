B4.5.32 RMI_PSMMU_ACTIVATE command
                 Activate a PSMMU.
                 The RMI_PSMMU_ACTIVATE command may initiate a Stateful RMI Operation whose context is bound to the
                 object identified by psmmu_ptr.
                 The RMI_PSMMU_ACTIVATE command may initiate a memory-transferring RMI Operation.
                 See also:
                    • A9.7 Physical SMMU
                    • A9.7.6 PSMMU interrupts
