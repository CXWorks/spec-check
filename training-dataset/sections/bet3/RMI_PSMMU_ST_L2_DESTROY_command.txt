B4.5.38 RMI_PSMMU_ST_L2_DESTROY command
                 Destroy a PSMMU Level 2 Stream Table.
                 The RMI_PSMMU_ST_L2_DESTROY command may initiate a Stateful RMI Operation whose context is bound
                 to the object identified by psmmu_ptr.
                 The RMI_PSMMU_ST_L2_DESTROY command may initiate a memory-transferring RMI Operation.
